Quick answers

You study mechanical. Why aerospace?+

Aerospace has always been a passion of mine. The University of Waterloo was my top choice of school, and Mechanical Engineering is the program there most closely related to aerospace.

Have you done real engineering work yet?+

Yes. At Clauger Canada I was lead project manager on a $150,000 compressor replacement, produced 9 AutoCAD drawings, and wrote a formal TSSA variance application.

Can you work in a shop?+

Yes. I've used a lathe, milling machine, drill press, and bandsaw, and I build my own prototypes with 3D printing and laser cutting.
